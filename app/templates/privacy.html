Pre-launch privacy notice

This notice describes the current public service. It will be updated before accounts, analytics, advertising attribution or commercial links are enabled.

Current public use

PrediBeacon does not currently offer public user accounts, deposits, trading, newsletters or affiliate tracking. The application does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. When a visitor chooses an external venue link, PrediBeacon records a first-party click identifier, market, campaign context when present, referring page and destination venue for security, performance measurement and future partner reconciliation. It does not record a trade or deposit.

Technical records

Hosting and security providers may process ordinary request data such as IP address, timestamp, requested path, user agent and error information to deliver and protect the service.

Administrative access

The private editorial interface keeps its token only in the current browser tab memory and uses no local storage, session storage or authentication cookie. Administrative actions are audited in the service database.

External sources

Opening a source or venue link sends the visitor to a third party governed by its own privacy terms. PrediBeacon does not control those services.

Future changes

Before enabling newsletters, personalized advertising, partner-paid attribution or accounts, PrediBeacon will document purpose, legal basis, retention, processors, international transfers and user rights.

Children

The service is not designed to encourage minors to participate in prediction markets. Future commercial features will require appropriate age and jurisdiction controls.
