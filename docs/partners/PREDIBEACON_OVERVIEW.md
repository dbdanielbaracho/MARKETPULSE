# PrediBeacon partner overview

Status: pre-launch working draft. PrediBeacon is the proposed public brand; MarketPulse remains the internal technical name. No partnership, endorsement, company registration, traffic level, or domain is claimed.

## Product

PrediBeacon is an independent prediction-market intelligence product. It currently:

- ingests up to 100 Kalshi and 100 Polymarket markets per refresh;
- keeps venue attribution, timestamps, source links, and freshness states;
- compares contracts without assuming that similar titles are equivalent;
- attaches official and independent evidence conservatively;
- creates AI-assisted drafts only behind evidence, cost, and human-review gates;
- stores approved publication versions with audited manual publish and rollback;
- does not accept deposits, custody funds, execute trades, or route orders;
- keeps automated publishing and social distribution disabled by default.

## Proposed relationship

The initial relationship should be limited to data/editorial/referral activity expressly authorized in writing. It should not include brokerage, order routing, custody, guaranteed returns, or claims of endorsement.

Items requiring written agreement:

1. permitted countries, states, audiences, and minimum age;
2. approved brand names, logos, market data, links, and disclosures;
3. organic, paid, influencer, affiliate, and referral rules;
4. compensation, attribution, reconciliation, refunds, and fraud handling;
5. review/approval process and prohibited claims;
6. incident contacts, suspension rights, termination, and content takedowns;
7. privacy, analytics, retention, and data-processing responsibilities.

## Safety controls

Country policy, platform authorization, partner contract, editorial approval, credential presence, and global kill switches are independent gates. Missing any required gate blocks commercial distribution. Brazil is informational-only by default.

## Evidence available

The repository contains automated tests, secret scanning, health/status endpoints, source telemetry, publication audit history, country policy, social readiness, risk, methodology, privacy, and terms pages.
