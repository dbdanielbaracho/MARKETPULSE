# Polymarket partnership proposal draft

Status: internal, unsent, pre-launch draft.

## Objective

Explore an authorized editorial, data, or referral relationship for PrediBeacon. The first phase should use public market information and tracked links only where contractually and geographically permitted.

## What PrediBeacon offers

- conservative discovery and comparison of Polymarket contracts;
- direct venue attribution, source links, freshness, deadlines, and rules;
- evidence-gated, human-reviewed educational explanations;
- safeguards against implying that similar markets are equivalent;
- country, credential, editorial, and partner-contract gates;
- audited manual publication and rollback.

## Proposed pilot

A 60- to 90-day non-exclusive editorial/data pilot:

- coverage limited to mutually approved categories and territories;
- tracked outbound links only after written authorization;
- no wallet custody, transaction signing, order routing, or execution;
- no paid promotion or influencer activity without separate approval;
- aggregate reporting subject to privacy thresholds;
- immediate suspension and takedown controls.

## Questions for Polymarket

1. Is the proposed relationship editorial/data, referral/affiliate, builder, or another program? These scopes must not be mixed.
2. Which territories, channels, age groups, market categories, and acquisition methods are allowed?
3. What brand, logo, disclosure, risk-text, review, and link requirements apply?
4. What constitutes an eligible referral, and what are the attribution, fraud, reconciliation, and payment terms?
5. What public API/data use, caching, latency disclosure, and rate limits apply commercially?
6. Are organic social, paid social, influencer, Telegram, WhatsApp, and short-form video treated differently?
