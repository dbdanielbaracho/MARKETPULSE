# Kalshi partnership proposal draft

Status: internal, unsent, pre-launch draft.

## Objective

Explore an authorized editorial, data, or referral relationship for PrediBeacon, an independent prediction-market intelligence product. The first phase should remain informational and avoid trade execution.

## What PrediBeacon offers

- conservative discovery and comparison of Kalshi markets;
- source attribution and direct links to the underlying contract;
- visible freshness, rules, deadlines, and non-equivalence warnings;
- evidence-gated, human-reviewed educational content;
- country and channel controls with distribution disabled until authorized;
- auditable publication and rollback.

## Proposed pilot

A 60- to 90-day non-exclusive pilot:

- editorial coverage of approved market categories;
- approved attribution and tracked outbound links;
- no custody, brokerage, order routing, or investment advice;
- no paid media or influencer activity until separately approved;
- weekly aggregate reporting after a minimum privacy threshold;
- immediate kill switch and takedown process.

## Questions for Kalshi

1. Which countries, US states, channels, age groups, and categories may be promoted?
2. Are organic social, paid social, influencers, and affiliate links separately authorized?
3. What exact risk text, brand assets, link format, and pre-approval workflow are required?
4. What attribution window, eligible action, fraud policy, reconciliation, and payment terms apply?
5. May PrediBeacon display cached prices or volume, and under what latency/attribution terms?
6. For Brazil, is any activity permitted directly, or only through a locally authorized relationship such as the publicly discussed XP route? Written confirmation is required before use.
