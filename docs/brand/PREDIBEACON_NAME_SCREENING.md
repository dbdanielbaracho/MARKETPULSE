# PrediBeacon name screening

Screening date: 2026-08-22. Status: preliminary only; not a legal opinion or trademark clearance.

## Result

An exact-phrase public web search did not reveal an obvious established product using the exact name PrediBeacon in the indexed results reviewed. Searches constrained toward USPTO and INPI also did not produce a conclusive registry record.

This result does **not** establish that the name is available. Search engines are incomplete, trademark rights may arise without a federal registration, similar-sounding marks can conflict, applications may be pending or poorly indexed, and rights differ by country and class.

## Required before launch investment

1. Search exact and phonetic variants in the USPTO Trademark Search system.
2. Search exact and similar variants in Brazil's INPI database.
3. Search relevant classes and related goods/services, including software, financial information, publishing, advertising, and education.
4. Search company registries, domains, app stores, social handles, and common-law usage.
5. Have qualified US and Brazilian trademark counsel assess likelihood of confusion and filing strategy.
6. Only then register the domain, file marks, commission final branding, or buy advertising.

Useful official starting points:

- USPTO trademark search information: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search
- INPI trademark services: https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/servicos/marcas

## Naming rule until cleared

Use “PrediBeacon” as a proposed working public brand only. Do not use ™ or ®, claim ownership/registration, or imply clearance. Keep the internal technical name MarketPulse unchanged unless engineering migration is later justified.
