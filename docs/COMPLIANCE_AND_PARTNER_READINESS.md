# PrediBeacon compliance and partner-readiness gates

This document is an operational control, not legal advice. It records conditions that must be satisfied before commercial outbound routing, paid social distribution or affiliate attribution can be enabled.

## Current launch posture

- Public brand: PrediBeacon
- Technical identifiers: MarketPulse / `MP_*`
- Initial editorial audience: US / global, English
- Brazil: informational content only
- Unknown country or eligibility: informational content only
- Public user accounts: disabled
- Custody, deposits and trade execution: outside current scope
- Automated website publishing: disabled
- Social distribution: disabled
- Commercial outbound routing: disabled
- Paid social advertising: disabled

## Partner contract gate

A partner route cannot be marked commercially verified until written terms identify:

- contracting legal entities;
- authorized products and venues;
- allowed and prohibited countries;
- minimum age and eligibility;
- approved destinations and domains;
- organic, paid, influencer and email permissions;
- required risk language;
- brand and trademark permission;
- commission basis, attribution window and currency;
- rejection, clawback, reversal and dispute rules;
- reporting and reconciliation evidence;
- privacy roles and international transfers;
- audit rights, suspension and termination;
- incident and regulatory-change contacts.

## Kalshi questions

- May PrediBeacon display and commercially link public Kalshi market data?
- Which countries may receive direct links?
- Must Brazilian residents be routed through XP International/Clear?
- Which marketing claims, age gates and risk statements are mandatory?
- Is organic election-market content treated differently from paid promotion?
- May PrediBeacon use Kalshi names, logos and screenshots?
- What affiliate, introducing-partner or media program is available?
- What event and reconciliation evidence will be supplied?

## Polymarket questions

- Which Polymarket service and terms apply to each country?
- May PrediBeacon use referral links for US or other eligible users?
- Which jurisdictions must be excluded?
- What is the approved distinction between Referral and Builder Programs?
- May PrediBeacon use names, logos, screenshots and market data commercially?
- What disclosures are required for pUSD, builder fees or rewards?
- How are clawbacks, linked accounts and inauthentic activity reported?

## Social channel gate

No provider may publish until all of the following are versioned and verified:

- channel owned by the business, with individual admin access and MFA;
- official API or approved provider credentials;
- platform and partner permission for the content class and target country;
- age, country and political-content controls;
- editorial approval and immutable source snapshot;
- sponsorship or affiliate disclosure;
- idempotency key and duplicate prevention;
- retry ceiling and permanent-failure queue;
- audit record containing payload digest, approval, response and timestamp;
- delete/withdraw action and global kill switch;
- no dependency from Market Core to a social provider.

## Prohibited marketing claims

- guaranteed profit, income or certainty;
- risk-free or loss-free participation;
- urgency fabricated to force a transaction;
- chasing losses or using essential-expense funds;
- claims directed to minors;
- omission of compensation or sponsorship;
- presenting market price as objective truth;
- implying PrediBeacon is a venue, broker, adviser or official partner;
- using third-party marks without permission;
- paid political or gambling-like promotion without written authorization.

## Release evidence

The final commercial launch audit must link each enabled country, partner and channel to:

1. contract evidence;
2. versioned policy;
3. configuration and kill switch;
4. automated tests;
5. platform approval;
6. live validation;
7. monitoring and rollback owner.
